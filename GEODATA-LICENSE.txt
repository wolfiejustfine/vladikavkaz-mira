Source identity, retrieval time and snapshot checksum are recorded in district.json
and district-report.json. district.geojson uses WGS84 longitude/latitude.
district.json uses the documented local metre coordinates and is not GeoJSON.

Heights and widths marked as estimates or derived values are assumptions added
by this project's conversion pipeline. They are not measurements from OSM.
Terrain, river-bank and bridge elevations have not been surveyed by this project.

This license statement applies to these geographic datasets, not the game code.
